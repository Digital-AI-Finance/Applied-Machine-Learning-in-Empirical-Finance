Project Timeline

  1. December 2025

    Project Launch

    Start of the industry PhD project at the University of Twente in collaboration with Quoniam Asset Management.

  2. December 2025

    Website Launch

    Launch of the project website with OpenAlex integration, the research agenda and team information.

  3. Start Q1 2026
    · Working Paper

    A Computational Pipeline for Systematic Literature Reviews in Empirical Finance: Methodology and Application to ML-Augmented Portfolio Allocation

    Development of a computational pipeline for systematic literature reviews, applied to machine learning in portfolio allocation. Literature synthesis in progress.

  4. Start Q2 2026
    · Working Paper

    When Disclosed Risks Materialize: Firm-Level Narrative Risk from 10-K Filings and Macro News

    Research on the relationship between firm-level risk disclosures in 10-K filings and macroeconomic news.

  5. September 2026
    · Conference

    3rd Conference on AI in Finance - Paper Development Workshop, TU Dresden

    Presentation of the working paper “When Disclosed Risks Materialize: Firm-Level Narrative Risk from 10-K Filings and Macro News” at the Paper Development Workshop.

  6. October 2026

    PhD Qualifier

    Formal assessment of research progress and plans for the remainder of the PhD at the University of Twente.

  7. 2027

    Core Research (Planned)

    Further investigation of risk management applications, development of new methods and preparation of additional publications.

  8. 2028-2029

    Thesis Completion (Planned)

    Thesis writing, preparation of final publications, industry validation and PhD defense.
